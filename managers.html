Currently In Charge

The two head coaches steering the rivalry into its next chapter.

Real Madrid — José Mourinho

  • AppointedSummer 2026
  • Previous clubBenfica

A legendary figure as the club’s manager, José Mourinho left an indelible mark on Real Madrid, winning the Copa del Rey and the historic 100-point La Liga title before continuing his career across Europe.

Barcelona — Hansi Flick

  • AppointedSummer 2024
  • Previous clubGermany national team
  • Signature trophy2020 Champions League (Bayern)

Flick won a historic sextuple with Bayern Munich in 2019/20 before taking over at Barcelona, immediately delivering a domestic treble in his first season and extending his contract through at least 2028.

Managers Who Coached Both Clubs

Crossing the touchline divide is almost as rare as crossing it as a player. Bernd Schuster, who played for both clubs, later managed Real Madrid (2007–2008) after a playing career that included both. Luis Enrique played for both Real Madrid and Barcelona before going on to manage Barcelona to a treble in 2015 — but never managed Madrid. No manager in the fixture's modern history has led both first teams; the rivalry's intensity makes it close to unthinkable.
