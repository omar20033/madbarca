What it means for the odds

Put together, that is why Barcelona are favourites: the better league record, home advantage at Camp Nou, a strong recent head-to-head and a Madrid side missing two of its main attackers. The draw is the least likely of the three results in the market, which fits two attacking teams that have both been scoring freely.

But it is not one-sided. Barcelona have just lost, are short of players and conceded four in Paris, while Madrid have won four in a row and found their scoring form. That is the case for the underdog, and it is why a Madrid win still carries a real probability in the market.

A caveat: form guides rest on small samples, and the market already reflects all of this. Treat it as context for the numbers, not as a prediction.

How to read these odds

Each percentage is the price of a Polymarket share in cents. A 61¢ share means traders collectively give that outcome roughly a 61% chance. The small number underneath is the same thing as decimal odds (1 divided by the probability).

The three result probabilities add up to slightly more than 100%. That small excess is the market’s built-in spread, not an error.

Prices move as traders react to team news and line-ups, so this page refreshes every 30 seconds. Markets with very little trading are hidden because their prices are not reliable.
