How to buy, what the official channels do, and what resale sellers are asking right now.

Read this first

The club has not put this match on general sale yet. General sale for big home games usually opens only days before kick-off, after members and Madridista Premium holders get their windows. Everything in the table below is resale, which is priced far above face value, and listings may be speculative this early. Real Madrid also restricts resale of its tickets, so check the seller’s guarantee and the ticket’s name-change rules before paying.

Kick-off time is not confirmed: sellers show anything from 15:00 to 21:00, so do not book flights or trains around a time you saw on a ticket site.

Safer ways to buy

Official first. Check the match page on realmadrid.com for the sale dates. Ordinary home games list general-public tickets from about €75; Clásico face value is much higher. VIP and hospitality packages are released earlier than normal tickets.

Resale second. Prefer sellers with a buyer guarantee and a clear refund policy, and avoid paying by bank transfer or to individuals.

Travelling from Barcelona? Book trains early; see our Where to Watch page if you decide to follow from home instead.
