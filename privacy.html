Privacy Policy

What data this site uses, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Who we are

madridvbarca.com ("MadridVBarca", "we", "us") is an independent fan website. You can contact us at kifeh.contact@gmail.com about anything in this policy.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not run forms that collect your name or email address. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

Like all websites, our hosting provider and the third-party services below can receive technical information automatically when you visit, such as your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you view.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit, which pages are popular and where visitors come from. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar technologies and collects usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser information. We use this data in aggregate to improve the site. Google processes this data under its own Privacy Policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Cookies

We do not set our own cookies and do not store our own data in your browser. Cookies and similar technologies may be set by the third-party services described on this page, including analytics and advertising partners. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Advertising

We may display advertising on this site provided by Mediavine through its Journey program. Ad partners may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies, and may collect information such as your IP address, browsing information and approximate location, to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure performance and, where permitted, personalise them based on your interests. Information about how Mediavine and its partners use data is available in Mediavine's privacy policy.

You can learn about and opt out of interest-based advertising from many companies through optout.aboutads.info (US), youronlinechoices.com (EU/UK) and your device settings. Visitors in the UK, EU and EEA can manage their consent choices where a consent tool is shown on the site.

Third-party services

  • Google Analytics (Google): site analytics, as described above.
  • Polymarket: our odds pages load live market prices directly from Polymarket's servers, so Polymarket can receive your IP address and browser details when those pages load. Links to Polymarket may contain a referral code and are marked as sponsored. Polymarket's own policies apply once you visit its site.
  • GitHub Pages: our site is hosted by GitHub, which may keep standard server logs.
  • Mediavine / Journey and its ad partners: advertising, as described above.

Legal bases (UK and EU visitors)

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, we rely on your consent for non-essential cookies and similar technologies (analytics and advertising), and on our legitimate interest in running and improving a free website for any server-level technical data. You can withdraw consent at any time through the consent controls on the site where shown, or through your browser settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold very little personal data ourselves, most requests relate to data held by Google and our advertising partners, who you can contact directly. UK visitors can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); EU visitors can contact their local data protection authority. California and other US state residents may have additional rights, including to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

International transfers

Google, Mediavine and other services may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows when it was last changed.
