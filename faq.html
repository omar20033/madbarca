FAQs

The questions people search for most about the Madrid–Barcelona rivalry, answered.

Who has more trophies, Real Madrid or Barcelona?

Real Madrid have the edge overall, with 106 official trophies to Barcelona's 104. Madrid lead internationally, largely on the strength of a record 15 Champions League/European Cup titles to Barça's 5. Barcelona lead domestically, mostly thanks to a record 32 Copa del Rey wins. See the full breakdown.

Which club is older, Real Madrid or Barcelona?

Barcelona is older. FC Barcelona was founded on 29 November 1899, while Real Madrid was founded in 1902 (as Madrid Football Club — it wasn't granted the "Real," or Royal, title until 1920).

Who has won more Clásicos?

It's dead even. Across 264 competitive meetings since 1902, Real Madrid have won 106 and Barcelona have won 106, with 52 draws. No side has ever held a clear historical edge in the fixture.

Why is it called "El Clásico"?

The name reflects the fixture's status as Spanish football's original and most historic rivalry — the two clubs met as early as 1902, and by the mid-20th century the match was already being described as the classic confrontation of Spanish football.

Who has won more La Liga titles?

Real Madrid, with 36 titles to Barcelona's 29 — a gap of seven. Madrid also hold the record for most consecutive titles (five, 1961–1965) and the highest points total in a single season.

Who has won more Champions League titles?

Real Madrid, by a wide margin — 15 titles to Barcelona's 5. Madrid are the all-time record holder in the competition, including winning five straight European Cups from 1956 to 1960 and four titles in five seasons between 2014 and 2018.

What's the biggest win in Clásico history?

Real Madrid's 11–1 win over Barcelona in a 1943 Copa del Generalísimo tie remains the largest margin of victory either side has ever managed in the fixture. Barcelona's biggest win came seven years later, a 7–2 victory in 1950.

Who won the last Clásico?

As of the end of the 2025/26 season, Barcelona won the most recent meeting 2–0 at Camp Nou on 10 May 2026 — a result that also clinched their second consecutive La Liga title. See the latest results for the full recent record.

Has any player played for both clubs?

Very few have crossed the divide directly given the rivalry's intensity, but it has happened — most notably Luis Figo's transfer from Barcelona to Real Madrid in 2000, which remains one of the most controversial moves in football history.
