Records & Extremes

Biggest Madrid Win

Real Madrid 11–1 Barcelona

Copa del Generalísimo (now Copa del Rey), 1943 — the largest margin of victory in the fixture's history.

Biggest Barça Win

Real Madrid 2–7 Barcelona

Copa del Generalísimo, 1950 — Barça's record margin over their rivals.

The highest-scoring Clásico of all came far earlier: a 6–6 draw in 1916. Real Madrid and Barcelona have combined for 106 trophies and 104 trophies respectively — see the full trophy breakdown.
