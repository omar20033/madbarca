So Who's Bigger?

It's closer than the rivalry's noise suggests. Real Madrid are the record holders in the competition that matters most internationally — 15 European Cups to Barça's 5 — and have more league titles overall. Barcelona are the undisputed kings of the Copa del Rey with 32 wins, and are the reigning back-to-back La Liga champions after beating Madrid to the title on the final stretch of 2025/26.

Domestically the two are separated by a handful of trophies out of well over 150 combined; internationally, Madrid's Champions League dominance opens a real gap. Head-to-head, in 264 Clásicos stretching back to 1902, neither side has ever pulled ahead — it's 106 wins apiece.
