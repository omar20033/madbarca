TV channels and live streams by country

LaLiga rights are sold country by country. Here is what we have confirmed for each region as of 25 October 2026, match day (UK and Spain based on broadcaster listings and press reports).

Middle East & North Africa

beIN Sports 1 HD

beIN Sports holds the exclusive LaLiga rights in 23 MENA markets, including Algeria, Morocco, Libya, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, the UAE, Kuwait and Jordan. All 380 matches are shown, with commentary in Arabic, English and French depending on where you are.

  • TV: beIN Sports 1 HD, with Arabic commentary (Issam Chaouali is reported as the commentator)
  • English commentary: beIN Sports English 1
  • Needs a beIN subscription
  • Stream on beIN SPORTS CONNECT if you have a package
  • Iraq: beIN shares the rights with the 1001 platform
  • Channel numbers can differ by provider — look for “beIN Sports 1” in your package
France

Disney+ (ESPN section)

Disney+ has shown all 380 LaLiga matches in France since the start of 2026/27, with ESPN coverage and French-language commentary. This Clásico is included in the subscription, with no separate sports add-on.

  • Stream: the Disney+ app, ESPN section (smart TV, internet box, mobile)
  • Kick-off: 21:00 French time
  • Based on French trade press — check in the app before kick-off
United States

ABC · ESPN+ · ESPN Deportes

ESPN presents this Clásico on ABC — the first time the fixture has aired on ABC in the US. A one-hour pre-match show starts at 3 p.m. ET.

  • TV: ABC, 4 p.m. ET
  • Stream: ESPN+ (English and Spanish) and the ESPN app with an ESPN Unlimited plan
  • Spanish-language TV: ESPN Deportes
United Kingdom

Premier Sports 1

Premier Sports shows La Liga in the UK, and TV listings have Barcelona vs Real Madrid on Premier Sports 1 at 20:00 UK time.

  • TV: Premier Sports 1
  • Stream: Premier Sports Player (listings also show Disney+)
  • Ireland: not confirmed — check your TV provider
Spain

DAZN LaLiga · Movistar Plus+

Spanish press and Movistar’s own guide put this Clásico on DAZN LaLiga. Movistar Plus+ and Orange TV carry the channel for customers whose package includes it. It is not on free-to-air TV.

  • TV: DAZN LaLiga (Movistar Plus+ dial 55; DAZN LaLiga 2 is on dial 58)
  • Stream: the DAZN app, or Movistar Plus+ / Orange TV if your package includes the channel
  • Not on free-to-air TV
Everywhere else

Check your local rights holder

LaLiga sells its rights separately in each country. Use LaLiga’s official website or your TV provider’s guide to find the channel in your country.

  • Official broadcasters only — unofficial streams are often unreliable and illegal

Frequently asked questions

What time is Barcelona vs Real Madrid on 25 October 2026?

Kick-off is at 9 p.m. CET (21:00) — that is 21:00 in Spain and Algeria, 20:00 in the UK and 4 p.m. Eastern Time in the US. See the table above for more countries.

Which channel shows Barcelona vs Real Madrid in the Arab world?

beIN Sports 1 HD, with Arabic commentary. beIN has the exclusive LaLiga rights in 23 MENA markets including Algeria, Morocco, Egypt and the Gulf states, and English commentary is on beIN Sports English 1. You need a beIN subscription, and you can also stream through beIN SPORTS CONNECT if your package includes it.

Where can I watch Barcelona vs Real Madrid in France?

On Disney+, in the ESPN section. Disney+ has shown all 380 LaLiga matches in France since the start of the 2026/27 season, with no separate sports add-on. Kick-off is at 21:00 French time.

Where can I watch El Clásico in the USA?

On ABC at 4 p.m. ET, with a pre-match show from 3 p.m. It is also on ESPN+ (English and Spanish), ESPN Deportes and the ESPN app for ESPN Unlimited subscribers.

Is there a free live stream of El Clásico?

In the US, ABC is broadcast television. In most other countries the official broadcasters are paid services. We do not link to unofficial streams, which are often unreliable and illegal.

Where is the match played?

At Camp Nou in Barcelona. Barcelona are the home team in this first Clásico of 2026/27; Real Madrid host the return fixture at the Santiago Bernabéu on 9 May 2027.

Which channel shows Barcelona vs Real Madrid in the UK?

Premier Sports 1, with kick-off at 20:00 UK time. You can stream it on Premier Sports Player, and listings also show Disney+.

Where can I watch Barcelona vs Real Madrid in Spain?

On DAZN LaLiga, a paid channel available on DAZN directly and through Movistar Plus+ (dial 55) and Orange TV packages that include it. It is not on free-to-air TV.
