Frequently asked questions

What time is Barcelona vs Deportivo Alavés on 31 October 2026?

Kick-off is at 21:00 Spain time on Saturday 31 October 2026 — that is 21:00 in Tunisia, Algeria and Morocco, 20:00 in the UK and 4 p.m. Eastern Time in the US. The match is played at Camp Nou.

What time is Racing Santander vs Real Madrid on 1 November 2026?

Kick-off is listed at 16:15 Spain time on Sunday 1 November 2026 — that is 16:15 in Tunisia, Algeria and Morocco, 15:15 in the UK and 10:15 a.m. Eastern Time in the US (US clocks go back that morning). La Liga can still move kick-off times, so check again closer to the date.

Which channel shows Barcelona and Real Madrid in the Arab world?

beIN Sports holds the La Liga rights across the Middle East and North Africa. The channel number (beIN Sports 1, 2, 3…) changes from match to match, so check beIN’s schedule on match day. You need a beIN subscription, and you can stream on beIN SPORTS CONNECT if your package includes it.

Where can I watch the matches in France?

On Disney+, in the ESPN section, with French commentary and no separate sports add-on.

Where can I watch the matches in the USA?

On ESPN+, and some matches are also on ESPN Deportes. The exact listing for each match is on ESPN’s schedule.

Where can I watch the matches in the UK?

On Premier Sports, which shows La Liga in the UK. You can stream it on Premier Sports Player.

Where can I watch the matches in Spain?

Each matchday is split between DAZN and Movistar Plus+ (Movistar LaLiga), and the channel for each match is announced a few days before. Orange TV carries both. La Liga is not on free-to-air TV.

Where are the matches played?

Barcelona host Deportivo Alavés at Camp Nou in Barcelona. Real Madrid play away at Racing Santander, at El Sardinero in Santander.

Is there a free live stream?

In most countries the official broadcasters are paid services. We do not link to unofficial streams, which are often unreliable and illegal.

When is the next El Clásico?

Real Madrid host Barcelona at the Santiago Bernabéu on 9 May 2027.
