About Us

An independent fan project comparing Real Madrid and FC Barcelona.

Why this site exists

MadridVBarca is an independent fan project built to answer the questions people keep asking about the biggest rivalry in club football: who has more trophies, who leads El Clásico, which players and managers defined each era, and where to watch the next match. We put the numbers for both clubs side by side in one place, in plain language, in English, Spanish, French and Arabic.

Who runs it

The site is run by an individual fan, not a company, club or media group. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Real Madrid CF, FC Barcelona, LaLiga, UEFA or any other official body. Club names and related marks belong to their respective owners and are used only to identify the clubs being discussed.

How the statistics are researched

Figures such as trophy counts, head-to-head results, records, transfers and finances are compiled from publicly available sources, including official club and competition records and respected football reference publications, and are cross-checked where sources disagree. Historical totals can differ between sources because of how competitions are counted, so where a number depends on a definition we try to say so on the page.

How often it is updated

We review the site regularly and update figures after major results and trophies. Around big fixtures such as El Clásico we add and refresh match-day pages, including where-to-watch and odds pages. Pages that show a date, such as the homepage, indicate when the data was last reviewed.
