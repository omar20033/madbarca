Great Eras

Every great club is really a string of great teams. Here are the ones that defined each side.

Real Madrid

1953–1964

Di Stéfano & Puskás

Alfredo Di Stéfano's arrival transformed Madrid into the dominant force of the European Cup's founding decade, partnered from 1958 by Ferenc Puskás.

5 straight European Cups (1956–60) · 8 La Liga titles
2000–2007

The Original Galácticos

Florentino Pérez's policy of signing a Ballon d'Or winner every summer brought Figo, Zidane, Ronaldo, Beckham and Owen into one dressing room.

2 La Liga titles · 1 Champions League (2002)
2013–2018

Ronaldo's Four Champions Leagues

With Cristiano Ronaldo at his peak and Zinedine Zidane arriving as coach in 2016, Madrid won an unprecedented three straight European titles.

4 Champions Leagues in 5 years · 1 La Liga
2021–present

Ancelotti's Second Coming

Carlo Ancelotti returned to steer Madrid through a generational handover — from Benzema's Ballon d'Or season to a rebuilt attack of Bellingham, Vinícius and Mbappé.

2 Champions Leagues · 2 La Liga titles (through 2023/24)

FC Barcelona

1988–1996

Cruyff's Dream Team

Johan Cruyff imported Total Football to Camp Nou, built around Guardiola, Koeman, Laudrup and Stoichkov, and won the club's first-ever European Cup.

4 straight La Liga titles · 1 European Cup (1992)
2004–2008

Ronaldinho's Rebuild

Frank Rijkaard rebuilt a struggling Barça around a young Ronaldinho, restoring the club to the top of Spanish and European football.

2 La Liga titles · 1 Champions League (2006)
2008–2012

Guardiola & The Sextuple

Pep Guardiola's tiki-taka side, built around a home-grown Messi–Xavi–Iniesta core, became the first European club to win six trophies in one year.

3 La Liga titles · 2 Champions Leagues · the 2009 Sextuple
2014–2017

MSN — Messi, Suárez, Neymar

Luis Enrique's front three became the most feared attack in world football, combining for over 350 goals across three seasons.

2 La Liga titles · 1 Champions League (2015) · a treble
2023–present

Flick's New Generation

After the post-Messi rebuild, Hansi Flick led a squad fronted by teenage sensation Lamine Yamal to back-to-back league titles.

Back-to-back La Liga titles (2024/25, 2025/26)
