Barcelona 1–2 Real Madrid: the numbers behind the Clásico

A first Madrid win at Camp Nou in almost three years, a landmark goal for Mbappé and a one-win lead in the all-time tally.

A few figures that put Sunday's 2–1 win for Real Madrid at Camp Nou in context.

107 vs 106. Heading into the match each club had 106 wins in the head-to-head. Madrid's victory moves them one clear on this site's tally, with 52 draws across 265 meetings.

October 2023. It was Real Madrid's first win at Camp Nou since October 2023, almost three years earlier.

100. Kylian Mbappé's winning goal was reported as his 100th for Real Madrid — a landmark reached in the closing minutes of a Clásico.

7 and 8. The two goals were Mbappé's seventh and eighth in the fixture.

4 wins, 2 draws. José Mourinho is now unbeaten in six Clásicos since returning as Madrid manager, with four wins and two draws.

3 points. Barcelona, Atlético Madrid and Real Madrid are separated by just three points at the top of La Liga after the weekend.
