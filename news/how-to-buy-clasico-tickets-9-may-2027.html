How to buy tickets for Real Madrid vs Barcelona on 9 May 2027

General sale has not opened. Here is the order in which tickets usually become available, and what to check before paying a reseller.

Real Madrid has not opened sales for the 9 May 2027 Clásico at the Santiago Bernabéu. For big home games the club usually sells first to members and Madridista Premium holders, and opens general sale only days before kick-off.

VIP and hospitality packages are normally released earlier than standard tickets. Ordinary league games list general-public seats from about €75; a Clásico costs far more at face value.

Resale sites already list the match, but those prices are well above face value and some listings may be speculative this early. Real Madrid also restricts resale, so check the seller’s guarantee and name-change rules. The kick-off time is still unconfirmed.
