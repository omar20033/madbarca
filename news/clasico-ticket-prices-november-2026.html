Clásico resale prices in November: cheapest seats start around $700

A 1 November snapshot of ten resale sellers for the 9 May 2027 match, with the cheapest and most expensive listings.

On 1 November 2026 the cheapest resale listings for Real Madrid vs Barcelona on 9 May 2027 started at about $706 on SeatPick and $717 at FootballTicketNet. Hellotickets began at $880 and VisitFootball at €999.

Further up the market the gap is huge: one aggregator showed an average listing of $2,485 and a top listing of $50,411, and a price tracker put the median at $2,700.

Prices move daily and sellers add fees at checkout, so compare the final total. None of these is an official price, and the club has not yet announced its own.
