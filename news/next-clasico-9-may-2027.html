Next Clásico: Real Madrid host Barcelona at the Bernabéu on 9 May 2027

The return league fixture falls three matchdays from the end of the season and could again decide the title.

The next meeting between the two clubs is the return league fixture on matchday 35 of La Liga, scheduled for the weekend of 8–9 May 2027 at the Santiago Bernabéu, with Real Madrid at home.

The date was set in La Liga's 2026/27 calendar. The exact kick-off time has not been announced yet, so the countdown on this site uses a placeholder time until it is confirmed.

The match falls just three matchdays from the end of the campaign, so it could again have a say in the title race — as the corresponding fixture did last season, when Barcelona beat Madrid at Camp Nou on 10 May 2026 to clinch the league.

The two clubs may also meet earlier in the Supercopa de España, if both qualify; semi-final pairings are drawn separately.
