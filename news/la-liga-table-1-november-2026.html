La Liga table: Barcelona lead by six, Real Madrid have a game in hand

Where the top of La Liga stands on Sunday 1 November, before Real Madrid’s game at Racing Santander.

On Sunday morning, before Real Madrid played at Racing Santander, Barcelona led La Liga with 30 points. Atlético Madrid were second on 28 after a 2–0 win at Levante on Saturday, Real Madrid third on 24 and Real Betis fourth on 23.

Madrid have played 10 games to Barcelona’s 11, so a win at Racing would cut the gap to three points.
