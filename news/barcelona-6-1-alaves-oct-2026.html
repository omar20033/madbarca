Barcelona thrash Alavés 6–1 six days after losing the Clásico

Barcelona answered the Clásico defeat with a 6–1 home win on Saturday night and stay top of La Liga.

Barcelona beat Deportivo Alavés 6–1 at Spotify Camp Nou on Saturday 31 October, six days after their 2–1 defeat by Real Madrid in the Clásico.

It was the team’s tenth league win in 11 matches and keeps Barcelona top of La Liga on 30 points, six ahead of Real Madrid, who have played a game fewer.
