Match report · 26 October 2026

Mbappé's late winner gives Real Madrid the Clásico at Camp Nou

Two goals from Kylian Mbappé — the second in the 87th minute — earned Real Madrid a 2–1 win and ended Barcelona's perfect start to the league season.

Real Madrid won the first Clásico of the 2026/27 season 2–1 at Spotify Camp Nou on Sunday night. Kylian Mbappé scored both goals, the second of them in the 87th minute, to end Barcelona's perfect start to the La Liga campaign.

Madrid went ahead in the 41st minute at the end of a patient move. Diomandé's cross into the box was met by Gerard Martín's attempted clearance, but the ball struck Mbappé and ended up in the net — a goal that owed as much to the deflection as to the build-up.

Barcelona levelled just after the hour. Pedri reversed a pass into Raphinha, whose low shot beat Andriy Lunin; the goalkeeper got a hand to it but could not keep it out. At 1–1 the home side pushed for a winner, but it was Madrid who found one.

With the game moving towards a draw, a flash of skill from Jude Bellingham opened the space and Eduardo Camavinga cut the ball back for Mbappé, who slotted it home. José Mourinho celebrated the goal sliding on his knees in front of the Madrid supporters.

It was Barcelona's first league defeat of the season — they had also lost to Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League five days earlier — and it leaves just three points between the top three: Barcelona, Atlético Madrid and Real Madrid.

On this site's all-competition count, the result puts Real Madrid ahead in the all-time head-to-head, 107 wins to 106, with 52 draws across 265 meetings. See the full Clásico record.
